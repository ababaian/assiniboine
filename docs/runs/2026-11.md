# The November 2026 run

The current screen of Logan's selfloops archive, run 2026-10-30 to
2026-11-03 with the method as documented in `docs/methods.md` and the
settings in `config/runs/2026-11.toml`. Compared with the
[October run](2026-10.md) it trims the 30 nt overlap, labels the closure
classes, applies the 200 nt floor, and does not deduplicate.

The code that ran it is the layout this repository had before its
refactoring for teaching (commit `105e72b` and earlier); the rules are
unchanged.

## Result

900,042,456 circles, 595,006,157 unnamed by core_nt, 161,292,329 screened,
**11,308,939 called rod** (7.01%), **43,887 carrying a ribozyme**,
**14,806 with ribozymes on both strands**, 47 of them corroborated.

Closure classes after stage 1: 562,743,416 circular (94.6%), 32,262,741

circular and 20,962,032 hairpin.

Shortlist: tier 1 is 47 rows; tier 2 kept 2,000 of a qualifying pool of
12,259, and the cut fell inside 2,210 rows tied at confidence 1.000, so
which 2,000 were kept is arbitrary.

## Observations

* The screened set is 70% smaller than October's, mostly from the 200 nt
  floor, but rods with a ribozyme fell only 5.5% (46,432 to 43,887).
* Hairpins are called rod at twice the circle rate (12.39% against
  6.21%), the excess entirely below 500 nt. Per chunk, the rod share runs
  from 0.02% to 22.83% and tracks the hairpin share (r = 0.79).
* Every one of October's 2,056 shortlisted circles is called rod again;
  2,052 keep a ribozyme and 1,097 are on this shortlist.
* Length distribution of the unclassified set: mean 243 nt, 96.9% at or
  under 500 nt, longest 437,661 nt. The 200 nt floor removed 73% of it.

## Compute

A single 480-core machine with 1.5 TB of memory. Kraken2 took 16 minutes
with ten processes of 32 threads sharing the memory-mapped 340 GB index.
The screen took about 66 hours with sixteen workers of 30 threads. Stage 7
took 2 h 47 min on 460 cores, about 1,000 CPU-hours. The run's exact
settings are `config/runs/2026-11.toml`.
